FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I volunteer with Krishna's Noble Foundation?

You can volunteer by contacting us through our form or calling our office. We welcome volunteers for various programs including education, healthcare camps, and food distribution.

How can I make a donation?

You can make donations through our donate page or contact us directly for bank transfer details. All donations are eligible for 80G tax exemption certificate.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) district in Maharashtra, focusing on rural and semi-urban areas with limited access to education and healthcare.

Can I visit your foundation center?

Yes, you're welcome to visit us during working hours (Mon-Sat, 9AM-6PM). We recommend scheduling an appointment by calling us in advance.
