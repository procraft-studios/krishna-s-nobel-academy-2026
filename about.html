Our Story

The Genesis of Krishna's Noble Foundation

Krishna's Noble Foundation (कृष्ण'स नोबल फाउंडेशन) was established with a vision to create meaningful change in the lives of the underprivileged. Based in the heart of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra, our organization has been at the forefront of social service since its inception.

Operating from our center at Plot No. 7, Opp Life Line Hospital, M2 Road, T.V. Center, Hudco, we have developed numerous programs that address the critical needs of education, healthcare, and nutrition in our community.

Our Mission

To uplift and empower underprivileged communities through comprehensive welfare programs focusing on education, healthcare, nutrition, and community development, creating a more equitable society.

  • Provide free education to needy children
  • Conduct regular medical camps
  • Ensure food security for families
  • Build community resilience

Our Vision

To create a world where every individual has access to basic necessities, quality education, and healthcare opportunities, regardless of their socio-economic background.

  • Zero poverty in our service areas
  • 100% literacy rate
  • Healthy and thriving communities
  • Self-sufficient families
